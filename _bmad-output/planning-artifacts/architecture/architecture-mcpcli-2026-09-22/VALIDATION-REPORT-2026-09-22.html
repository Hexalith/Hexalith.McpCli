Executive decision

The spine is mechanically clean and strong on its central hexagonal boundary, catalog ownership, executor sequencing, dependency closure, packaging direction, and v1 runtime envelope. It still conflicts with the binding PRD on idempotency and public documents, specifies two unimplementable integration-test guarantees, relies on a nonexistent composition package, and leaves several head/executor/release seams open to divergent implementations.

Implementation: hold story breakdown and implementation that depends on this spine. Resolve all four Critical and seven High findings first; incorporate the Medium/Low changes and decisions in the same update, or explicitly defer only where a finding permits it with an owner, operating constraint, and revisit gate. Then rerun the complete gate.

Release: do not approve a v1 release plan from the current spine. Its harness and release transaction do not yet prove that the promised artifacts and cross-head behavior can be produced.

Mutation statement: this validation did not change ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md, .memlog.md, the PRD, its addendum, or any source review.

VAL-001 · The spine generates an idempotency key that the final PRD forbids

Critical
Source-review IDs
RV-RUB-001
Exact evidence
ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:83,107,189 makes the key unconditional and generated. The binding contract makes it optional and caller-supplied only at ../../prds/prd-mcpcli-2026-09-21/prd.md:103-105,338-354,523-532 and ../../prds/prd-mcpcli-2026-09-21/addendum.md:192-196,277-283.
Why it matters
Executor stories can implement incompatible retry and payload-fill semantics, and a generated key weakens the no-generic-safe-retry guardrail.
Recommended disposition
Autofix. Generate only MessageId; use it as omitted correlation ID; keep IdempotencyKey absent unless supplied, including Payload overwrite and result serialization.
