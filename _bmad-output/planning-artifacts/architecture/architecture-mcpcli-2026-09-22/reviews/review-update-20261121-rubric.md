# Architecture rubric review · 21 November 2026

**Verdict.** Pass after one correction to AD-9. The feature spine gives the CLI, MCP, Core, module owners and release owners enforceable boundaries. The review covered the 21 November update against McpCli `e79b7f8b`, its requirements and the refreshed producer and Platform decisions.

**Resolved finding · AD-9.** The draft promised one submission for every invocation and described the decision as preventing all retries. Both preflight refusal and operator recovery require a more precise rule. The revised text permits at most one Gateway submission per invocation and requires zero when preflight refuses the call. Either Head and Core must avoid automatic retries. A caller facing an unknown outcome first needs confirmation of a trusted idempotency adapter for that Command type on its target deployment. This matches the executor's command and query branches and the normative result contract. **Enrollment and adoption.** The production project enrolls Parties, Tenants and Projects. AD-15 and AD-23 identify the published Folders artifacts, the producer's outstanding Command and Query work and the restriction on the mixed C10 operation. Implementers have an explicit owner and evidence requirement for each step from publication to enrollment, live qualification and retirement. The approved AD-23 records retain their original scope.
